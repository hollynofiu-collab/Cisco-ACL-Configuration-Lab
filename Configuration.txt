! ==========================================
! Cisco ACL Configuration
!ACL 100
! ==========================================

! Deny VLAN 20 from accessing VLAN 40
access-list 100 deny ip 192.168.20.0 0.0.0.255 192.168.40.0 0.0.0.255

What it means: Block IP traffic originating from 192.168.20.0/24 network and destined for the 192.168.40/24 network.


! Permit all other traffic
access-list 100 permit ip any any

! Apply ACL to the appropriate interface
interface FastEthernet0/0.2
ip access-group 100 in

VERIFICATION.

TEST 1

Source: VLAN 20
Destination: VLAN 40
EXPECTED: Denied.

TEST 2

Source: VLAN 20
Destination: VLAN 10
Expected: Permitted.
